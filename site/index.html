05 / What we found, honestly

Our first idea failed.

Phone signals picked the week to ask no better than chance. So we rebuilt Steady to ask first, and trained a model on students' own answers to decide when. Every method below gets the same 12 asks a year.

Limits

  • The self-harm phrase list missed 9 of 15 test messages.
  • The ask model still missed 586 of 2,171 high weeks, and 2,012 of its asks landed on weeks that were not high.
  • A model on one check-in worked better for men (AUC 0.75) than for women (0.66).
  • Every student was in college, not high school.
  • Next: a fine-tuned language model found 62% of low-mood comments, against 50%. It is 91 MB and flags dark jokes, so it is not in the app yet.
