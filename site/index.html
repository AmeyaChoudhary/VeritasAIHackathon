02 / What Stability does

  1. 01

    Sees her digital life

  2. 02

    Checks in

  3. 03

    Asks sooner

  4. 04

    Brings in a parent

With consent from Maya and her parents: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, browsing, newsfeeds, Spotify, games and chats. Parents never see a message, search or video.

Meet Stable. Once a week it checks in: two taps, for stress and time with people.

Every month, the two questions doctors use. In week 4 Maya scores 2 of 6. In finals her stress goes from 2 to 4, and the model asks two weeks early.

Week 8: she scores 4, then 12 on all eight, past the cutoff of 10. A nudge to see a doctor, and a short note to her mom.

Maya's phone

03 / Privacy

Her words never leave her phone.

Small models read what she types, watches and searches, keep only numbers, and delete the words. Words about self-harm bring up 988, one tap away.

Each dot is one message. The words are gone.

0 bytes sent

Scored on the device
